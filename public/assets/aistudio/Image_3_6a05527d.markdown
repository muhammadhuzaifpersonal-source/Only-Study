## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the regal grandeur of Kashmir’s alpine valleys fused with the high-octane theatricality of elite world cricket and international high-performance sportswear. It targets visionary athletes, collectors, franchise patrons, and modern sports enthusiasts who view athletic culture through the lens of luxury, craft, and prestige.

The emotional signature is intensely atmospheric, commanding, and cinematic:
- **Atmospheric Grandeur:** Evoking the tension of floodlit nocturnal cricket arenas framed against crisp mountain air.
- **Editorial High-Performance:** Merging the razor-sharp functional minimalism of elite apparel labs (e.g., NikeLab, Rapha) with the ornate trophy-room reverence of heritage cricket.
- **Electric Momentum:** Tactile, glowing accents punctuate vast nocturnal canvases, creating immediate focus during real-time gameplay, auctions, and exclusive drops.

The style is an exacting hybrid of **Cinematic Dark-Mode Luxury** and **Glassmorphic Precision**. Deep obsidian and midnight graphite planes provide infinite depth, while brushed metallic gold and energetic sports emerald accents serve as precise, glowing signifiers of victory, status, and live match momentum.

## Colors

The palette operates in high-contrast nocturnal tiers, deliberately engineered for low-light legibility, broadcast-level drama, and unmistakable luxury.

- **Primary (`#00E676` - Stadium Green):** The electric heartbeat of the brand. Used sparingly for live match telemetry, primary interactive states, match victory markers, and high-impact micro-accents.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Brushed Gold / Warm Amber):** Denotes prestige, premier tiering, championship trophies, and VIP community badging.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Alpine Spruce):** Grounded emerald providing depth in duotone surface treatments, card borders under hover, and performance status tags.
- **Base Canvas (`#0A0D0F` - Midnight Pitch):** Deepest background tone evoking midnight floodlight shadows.
- **Neutral Surface (`#0E1317` - Obsidian Layer):** Primary container and structural surface background, providing low-frequency contrast against the pitch background.
- **Support Tones:** Crisp icy white (`#F8FAFC`) reserved for primary editorial headlines and live numeric tallies; muted slate (`#64748B`) for statistical data metadata and passive strokes.

## Typography

The typographic strategy juxtaposes structural high-fashion weight with athletic analytical clarity.

- **Headlines & Expressive Display (Syne):** Provides an avant-garde, sculptural silhouette. Its broad proportions, sharp geometric terminals, and commanding high weights mirror the physical drama of professional stadium arenas and luxury apparel branding.
- **Body & Data Telemetry (DM Sans):** Delivers uncompromised legibility across complex statistical matrices, live score trackers, editorial roster narratives, and technical apparel specifications.
- **Monogram and Numbers:** Numeric performance statistics leverage Syne’s tight, deliberate numerals to deliver immediate impact in high-speed mobile glance views.
- **Uppercase Hierarchy:** All category tags, player positions, match states (e.g., `LIVE`, `INNINGS BREAK`), and micro-labels must be styled using `label-caps` with wide letter-spacing (`0.12em`) to maintain an authoritative military-athletic hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display large-format editorial photography, real-time stadium metrics, and bespoke merchandise grids.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column layout with 48px outer canvas margin, 24px column gutters, and a maximum central content constraint of 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 32px outer canvas margin, 20px column gutters.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout with 20px outer canvas margin, 16px column gutters. High-density stats reflow from side-by-side matrices into horizontal scroll snapping rails.

### Spacing Principles
- Spacing follows an intentional rhythmic cadence where major narrative panels have expansive breathing room (`space-xl` and higher), reflecting the confidence of elite luxury fashion houses.
- Interior card components and real-time scoreboard modules use tighter structural tokens (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to ensure critical telemetry remains compact, actionable, and scan-friendly without breaking visual composure.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical stadium darkness, tonal glass stratification, and luminous edge lighting rather than standard drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Arena Surface):** Raw midnight graphite canvas (`#0A0D0F`), flat and light-absorbing.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Solid obsidian tone (`#0E1317`) reinforced by a micro-border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` to establish physical containment without visual clutter.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action & Navigational Layers):** Glassmorphic frosted surfaces using `rgba(14, 19, 23, 0.75)` with a backdrop blur of `24px`. Edge borders use a top-weighted directional linear gradient fading from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Critical Focus):** Elevated obsidian container surrounded by a diffused, tinted halo shadow: `0 20px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 30px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.08)`.
- **Match Glow Signifiers:** Live events, winning moments, and active states emit a localized radial bloom of Stadium Green (`#00E676`) with a 32px to 48px blur at 15% opacity, simulating the incandescent glow of stadium floodlights through nocturnal mist.

## Shapes

The design system employs a signature **28px curvature** for outer structural elements, framing components with the sculptural finish of high-end aerodynamic athletic equipment and pristine composite materials.

- **Luxury Sports Cards & Major Modals:** Anchored with an exaggerated outer radius of 28px (`rounded-xl` equivalent in component specifications) to establish an unmistakable, high-prestige product identity.
- **Standard Controls & Interactive Trays:** Base elements utilize `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px), preventing smaller touch targets from feeling excessively bubbly or non-functional.
- **Tags & Status Pills:** Full pill treatment (9999px) for live badges, tier indicators, and player tags, offering contrast against the larger architectural radius of the cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Athletic CTA:** Solid Stadium Green (`#00E676`) fill with ultra-dark obsidian typography (`#0A0D0F`), bold weight (`fontWeight: 700`), 12px vertical padding, 28px horizontal padding, and a micro corner radius of 12px. On hover, it produces an intensified emerald ambient bloom (`0 0 24px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Prestige CTA:** Deep graphite fill (`#151B20`), 1px gold metallic border (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`), gold typography (`#F59E0B`), transforming on hover to a semi-translucent amber glow.
- **Ghost Utility Action:** Transparent background with subtle icy white borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) and white text, scaling slightly (1.02x) on active touch.

### Luxury Sports Cards
- Constructed with obsidian background (`#0E1317`), an outer radius of 28px, and an interior padding of 24px.
- Encased in a hair-thin perimeter outline (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Hover states initiate a top-down gradient highlight and lift the card vertically by 4px, accompanied by a subdued emerald or gold outline illumination depending on franchise classification.

### Chips & Match Badges
- **Live Match Pill:** 9999px pill radius, obsidian glass fill (`rgba(10, 13, 15, 0.8)`), 1px solid border of `#00E676`. Features a synchronized pulsing dot icon (`#00E676`, 6px diameter) alongside uppercase tracking typography.
- **Trophy & Franchise Tier Badges:** Warm amber tinted surface (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`), brushed gold label (`#F59E0B`), and hairline gold borders.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed midnight base (`#07090B`) with an internal inset shadow creating functional depth.
- Border is a muted slate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), transitioning to focused Stadium Green (`#00E676`) with a 0 0 12px green diffusion on focus.
- Placeholder text is set in muted slate (`#64748B`), and active inputs display crisp icy white typography (`#F8FAFC`).

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist graphite frames. When selected, they fill with solid `#00E676` featuring an obsidian checkmark or inner core, accompanied by a subtle 4px neon blur.
- **Switch Toggles:** Heavy obsidian pill track (44px x 24px) with a floating metallic thumb. Active state slides right, shifting the track background from graphite to deep emerald (`#059669`).

### Live Telemetry & Scoreboards
- Dark glassmorphic floating bar housing live run rates, pitch metrics, and bowler analytics.
- Distinct split-flap style visual divisions between innings, framed with subtle 1px vertical borders and illuminated numeric typography.
